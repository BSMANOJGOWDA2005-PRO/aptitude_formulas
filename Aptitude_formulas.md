# 📚 Aptitude Formulas & Examples

A collection of important aptitude formulas with simple examples for **placement exams, interviews, and competitive exams**.

---

## 1. Percentage

### Formula

```text
Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Percentage Increase = ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100

Percentage Decrease = ((Old Value - New Value) / Old Value) × 100
```

### Example

A student scores 45 marks out of 60.

```text
Percentage = (45 / 60) × 100
           = 75%
```

**Answer: 75%**

---

## 2. Profit and Loss

### Formulas

```text
Profit = SP - CP

Loss = CP - SP

Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100

Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100

SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) / 100

SP = CP × (100 - Loss%) / 100
```

Where:

- CP = Cost Price
- SP = Selling Price

### Example

CP = ₹500  
SP = ₹650

```text
Profit = 650 - 500
       = ₹150

Profit % = (150 / 500) × 100
         = 30%
```

**Answer: 30% profit**

---

## 3. Simple Interest

### Formula

```text
SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Amount = P + SI
```

Where:

- P = Principal
- R = Rate of interest
- T = Time

### Example

P = ₹1000  
R = 10%  
T = 2 years

```text
SI = (1000 × 10 × 2) / 100
   = ₹200

Amount = 1000 + 200
       = ₹1200
```

**Answer: SI = ₹200**

---

## 4. Compound Interest

### Formula

```text
Amount = P(1 + R/100)^T

CI = Amount - P
```

### Example

P = ₹1000  
R = 10%  
T = 2 years

```text
Amount = 1000(1 + 10/100)^2
       = 1000(1.1)^2
       = ₹1210

CI = 1210 - 1000
   = ₹210
```

**Answer: CI = ₹210**

## 5. Average

### Formula

```text
Average = Sum of Observations / Number of Observations
```

### Example

Numbers = 60, 70, 80

```text
Average = (60 + 70 + 80) / 3
        = 210 / 3
        = 70
```

**Answer: 70**

## 6. Ratio and Proportion

### Formula

```text
a : b = a / b

If a : b = c : d

Then:

a × d = b × c
```

### Example

The ratio of boys to girls is 2 : 3.

Total students = 50.

Total parts:

```text
2 + 3 = 5
```

One part:

```text
50 / 5 = 10
```

Therefore:

```text
Boys = 2 × 10 = 20
Girls = 3 × 10 = 30
```

**Answer: Boys = 20, Girls = 30**

## 7. Time and Work

### Formula

If A completes a work in `x` days:

```text
A's 1-day work = 1/x
```

If A and B complete work in `x` and `y` days:

```text
Together = (x × y) / (x + y)
```

### Example

A completes a work in 10 days.  
B completes the same work in 15 days.

```text
Together = (10 × 15) / (10 + 15)
         = 150 / 25
         = 6 days
```

**Answer: 6 days**

## 8. Pipes and Cisterns

### Formula

```text
Filling rate = 1/x

Emptying rate = 1/y

Net rate = Filling rate - Emptying rate
```

### Example

A pipe fills a tank in 12 hours.  
Another pipe empties it in 18 hours.

```text
Net rate = 1/12 - 1/18

         = 3/36 - 2/36

         = 1/36
```

Therefore, the tank fills in:

```text
36 hours
```

**Answer: 36 hours**

## 9. Time, Speed and Distance

### Formulas

```text
Speed = Distance / Time

Distance = Speed × Time

Time = Distance / Speed
```

### Speed Conversion

```text
1 km/hr = 5/18 m/s

1 m/s = 18/5 km/hr
```

### Example

Distance = 120 km  
Time = 2 hours

```text
Speed = 120 / 2
      = 60 km/hr
```

**Answer: 60 km/hr**

## 10. Trains

### Formulas

Train crossing a pole:

```text
Time = Length of Train / Speed
```

Two trains crossing each other:

```text
Time = Sum of Train Lengths / Relative Speed
```

### Example

Train length = 200 m  
Speed = 20 m/s

```text
Time = 200 / 20
     = 10 seconds
```

**Answer: 10 seconds**

## 11. Boats and Streams

### Formulas

```text
Downstream Speed = Boat Speed + Stream Speed

Upstream Speed = Boat Speed - Stream Speed

Boat Speed = (Downstream + Upstream) / 2

Stream Speed = (Downstream - Upstream) / 2
```

### Example

Downstream = 12 km/hr  
Upstream = 8 km/hr

```text
Boat Speed = (12 + 8) / 2
           = 10 km/hr

Stream Speed = (12 - 8) / 2
             = 2 km/hr
```

**Answer:**

- Boat speed = 10 km/hr
- Stream speed = 2 km/hr

## 12. Probability

### Formula

```text
Probability = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes
```

### Example

A standard die has 6 faces.

Probability of getting 4:

```text
P(4) = 1 / 6
```

**Answer: 1/6**

## 13. HCF and LCM

### Formula

For two numbers:

```text
HCF × LCM = Product of the Two Numbers
```

### Example

Numbers = 12 and 18

```text
HCF = 6
LCM = 36

HCF × LCM = 6 × 36
           = 216

12 × 18 = 216
```

Therefore:

```text
HCF × LCM = Product
```

## 14. Permutation

### Formula

```text
nPr = n! / (n-r)!
```

### Example

Find `5P2`.

```text
5P2 = 5! / (5-2)!

    = 5! / 3!

    = (5 × 4 × 3!) / 3!

    = 20
```

**Answer: 20**

---

## 15. Combination

### Formula

```text
nCr = n! / [r!(n-r)!]
```

### Example

Find `5C2`.

```text
5C2 = 5! / (2! × 3!)

    = (5 × 4) / (2 × 1)

    = 10
```

**Answer: 10**

## 16. Geometry

### Rectangle

```text
Area = Length × Breadth

Perimeter = 2(Length + Breadth)
```

### Square

```text
Area = Side²

Perimeter = 4 × Side
```

### Triangle

```text
Area = 1/2 × Base × Height
```

### Circle

```text
Area = πr²

Circumference = 2πr
```

### Example

Rectangle:

Length = 8 cm  
Breadth = 5 cm

```text
Area = 8 × 5
     = 40 cm²
```

**Answer: 40 cm²**

## 17. Quadratic Equation

### Formula

For:

```text
ax² + bx + c = 0
```

The roots are:

```text
x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a
```

### Example

```text
x² - 5x + 6 = 0
```

Here:

```text
a = 1
b = -5
c = 6
```

Therefore:

```text
x = (5 ± √(25 - 24)) / 2

x = (5 ± 1) / 2
```

So:

```text
x = 3
x = 2
```

**Answer: x = 2 or 3**

## 18. Arithmetic Progression (AP)

### Formula

```text
nth term = a + (n-1)d
```

Sum of n terms:

```text
Sn = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]
```

Where:

- `a` = First term
- `d` = Common difference
- `n` = Number of terms

### Example

Sequence:

```text
2, 5, 8, 11, ...
```

Find the 5th term.

```text
a = 2
d = 3
n = 5

5th term = 2 + (5-1) × 3
         = 2 + 12
         = 14
```

**Answer: 14**

---

## 19. Geometric Progression (GP)

### Formula

```text
nth term = ar^(n-1)
```

Where:

- `a` = First term
- `r` = Common ratio

### Example

Sequence:

```text
2, 4, 8, 16, ...
```

Find the 5th term.

```text
a = 2
r = 2
n = 5

5th term = 2 × 2^(5-1)
         = 2 × 2^4
         = 32
```

**Answer: 32**

## 20. Clock

### Formula

```text
Angle = |30H - 5.5M|
```

Where:

- H = Hour
- M = Minutes

### Example

Find the angle at 3:00.

```text
Angle = |30 × 3 - 5.5 × 0|
      = |90|
      = 90°
```

**Answer: 90°**

## 21. Ages

### Basic Formula

```text
Future Age = Present Age + Number of Years

Past Age = Present Age - Number of Years
```

### Example

Rahul is 20 years old.

After 5 years:

```text
20 + 5 = 25 years
```

Five years ago:

```text
20 - 5 = 15 years
```

**Answer: Future = 25 years, Past = 15 years**

## 22. Mixtures and Alligation

### Basic Formula

```text
Mean Price =
(Q1 × P1 + Q2 × P2) / (Q1 + Q2)
```

Where:

- Q = Quantity
- P = Price

### Example

Mix 2 kg of rice at ₹40/kg with 3 kg at ₹60/kg.

```text
Mean Price = (2 × 40 + 3 × 60) / (2 + 3)

           = (80 + 180) / 5

           = 260 / 5

           = ₹52/kg
```

**Answer: ₹52/kg**

## 23. Partnership

### Formula

```text
Profit Share ∝ Capital × Time
```

### Example

A invests ₹10,000 for 12 months.  
B invests ₹20,000 for 6 months.

```text
A's ratio = 10000 × 12 = 120000

B's ratio = 20000 × 6 = 120000
```

Therefore:

```text
A : B = 1 : 1
```

**Answer: Profit is divided equally.**

## 24. Number System

### Sum of first n natural numbers

```text
Sum = n(n+1) / 2
```

### Sum of squares

```text
Sum = n(n+1)(2n+1) / 6
```

### Sum of cubes

```text
Sum = [n(n+1)/2]²
```

### Example

Find the sum of first 10 natural numbers.

```text
Sum = 10(10+1) / 2
     = 10 × 11 / 2
     = 55
```

**Answer: 55**

## 25. Logarithms

### Formulas

```text
log(ab) = log(a) + log(b)

log(a/b) = log(a) - log(b)

log(a^n) = n log(a)
```

### Example

```text
log(100) = log(10²)

         = 2 log(10)

         = 2
```

**Answer: 2**
